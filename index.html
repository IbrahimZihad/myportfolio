About

Who I Am

I'm Md. Ibrahim Zihad — a full-stack web developer who builds products that actually ship, not demos that stop at the mockup. I take an idea from database schema to deployed interface and stay accountable for it in production.

That means live systems for real clients: a Swedish salon booking platform with a concurrency-safe availability engine, a bilingual restaurant POS and PWA, and a digital subscription store handling real payments. Different stacks, same discipline — ship it, secure it, keep it running.

My sweet spot is the seam between frontend and backend: React, Next.js and TypeScript on the surface; Node, Express, NestJS or Django underneath; MySQL, MongoDB, Prisma and Sequelize holding it together. I care about the parts users never see — payment webhooks, role-based auth, double-booking prevention, tests that catch what manual QA misses.

I graduated from United International University with a BSc in CSE, majoring in Software Engineering, at 3.93 / 4.00 CGPA. But the projects below are the real proof — keep scrolling, they say more than a summary can.
